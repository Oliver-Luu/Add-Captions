# Add Captions help

## Captioning a video

1. Drop a video on the window, or click **Choose Video…** (File > Open Video…, ⌘O).
2. Wait while the app listens. The first time, your device may download Apple's English speech model.
   A one-hour video usually takes a minute or two on an M-series Mac.
3. Check the captions. Click a caption's time to jump there; click its text to fix a word. The caption
   showing in the video is highlighted, and the list follows the video while it plays.
4. Click **Save** (or use the File menu) and pick a format. When it's ready, choose **Save to…** to pick
   a folder, or **Share** (on iPhone and iPad, Share > Save Video puts it in Photos).

## Editing captions

- **Fix text**: click the text and type. A caption is wrapped onto two lines automatically.
- **Fix timing**: play or click to the right moment, then use the **⋯** menu on the caption:
  *Start Caption at Playhead* or *End Caption at Playhead*.
- **Add a caption**: move to where it should start and click **+** above the list.
- **Delete a caption**: **⋯** > *Delete Caption*. Empty captions are left out when you save.

## Which format should I save?

- **Burned in**: always visible, works everywhere (Instagram, TikTok, presentations). Can't be turned
  off. Takes longest, because the whole video is re-encoded.
- **Subtitle track**: viewers turn captions on in their player. Fast, and the picture is untouched.
  QuickTime, Photos, VLC and Apple devices show it; many websites don't.
- **.srt / .vtt**: just the captions. Upload the .srt next to your video on YouTube or Vimeo, or import
  it into a video editor.

## Caption style (burned-in only)

**Caption Style** in the toolbar sets the size, whether captions sit at the bottom or top, and whether
they have a dark box (readable on anything) or an outline (covers less of the picture). The preview
shows exactly what will be saved.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No speech was heard"**: the video has no talking, or the sound is too quiet or noisy.
- **"This video has no sound to caption"**: the file has no audio track.
- **Captions are in the wrong language or garbled**: the app only recognizes English.
- **A word is wrong**: fix it in the list; speech recognition is good but not perfect, especially with
  names and technical terms.
- **Privacy**: everything happens on your device. The video is never uploaded.
